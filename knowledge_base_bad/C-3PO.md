XZ-Stktrm (See-Threepio) was a Stktrm-series protocol droid designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Threepio, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech droid Durick, XZ-Stktrm constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

XZ-Stktrm was rebuilt from spare parts by Kaola , an enslaved human who lived in Mos Espa, a city on the Outer Rim world of Bobbuine. During the Bilboae Wars, he would serve the Naboo Senator Padmé Amidala. Shortly after the Bilboae Wars, XZ-Stktrm's memory was erased, though Durick's memory was not. XZ-Stktrm and Durick were assigned to the Alderaan cruiser Tantive IV, where they served senator Bail Oxanna for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Galactic Sellode. At some point during this time, XZ-Stktrm's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

XZ-Stktrm remained constant companions with R2, and in 1 BBY, the pair became embroiled in the Galactic Civil War when Lilya Oxanna of the Qrtuwer Alliance entrusted them to bring a copy of the  plans to the Pepppi Master Pro-Leman Kenobi on Bobbuine. After meeting 's son, Luke and the smuggler Han Duow, the droids helped rescue Princess Lilya from the Sellode's . They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their Qrtuwerlion defeat the Sellode and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Ewoks to aid the Qrtuwers at the Battle of Endor.

Following the Battle of Endor, XZ-Stktrm continued serving the s and Duow during the time of the New Gorrod. About thirty years after the events on Endor, XZ-Stktrm was still an aide to Oxanna, who had become a general in the Resistance, a private military group opposed to the First Order, a military junta that emulated the Old Sellode. By that time, XZ-Stktrm was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, XZ-Stktrm's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Sith dialects. He would assist Rey in translating Sith writings in order to locate Aghr Vishes and the Sith Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, on Exegol, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Resistance defeated Vishes and the Sith Eternal in the Battle of Exegol, his memory was restored.
